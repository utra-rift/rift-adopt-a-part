RIFT is a new robotics team at the University of Toronto, building a combat robot for ARC: two machines, 17 mm projectiles, two minutes.

We haven’t built it yet, but we know every part it needs. The Skule Endowment Fund has already adopted most of the hardware (shown in grey). What’s left is the chassis, a few parts, and getting the team to the competition.
